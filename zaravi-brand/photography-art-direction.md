# ZARAVI PHOTOGRAPHY & ART DIRECTION SYSTEM
*Complete Visual Universe — Territory B: Dark Romantic / Editorial / Couture*

---

## PHOTOGRAPHY PHILOSOPHY

ZARAVI photography does not document jewelry. It **stages transformation**.

Every image answers: *What does it feel like to become?*

**Core Principles:**
- Light emerges from shadow (never flat illumination)
- Imperfection is intentional (not flaw, but evidence of life)
- The wearer is protagonist, not mannequin
- Environment is atmosphere, not backdrop
- The butterfly is felt, not always seen

## THREE VISUAL FAMILIES

### FAMILY A: PRODUCT — "The Specimen"

**Purpose:** Commercial clarity with editorial soul

**Lighting:**
- Single large source (4×6 ft softbox or north window)
- Position: 45° above, 30° off-axis
- Fill: White reflector opposite (2:1 ratio)
- No ring lights, no on-camera flash
- Catchlights: Single, rectangular, in upper iris of gemstones

**Camera:**
- Medium format (100MP+) or high-res full frame (60MP+)
- Lens: 120mm macro (equiv.) — compression without distortion
- Aperture: f/11-f/16 (full depth) / f/2.8-f/4 (selective)
- Focus stacking: 15-30 frames for hero detail shots
- Tripod: Always. Remote trigger. Mirror lockup.

**Background:**
- Dark espresso velvet (zero reflection)
- Warm ivory matte paper (subtle texture)
- Sage uncoated paper (collection pages only)
- Never seamless white, never pure black

**Composition:**
- Centered symmetry (butterfly principle)
- Generous negative space (3:1 space:product)
- Scale reference: Always one shot with human element (hand, neck, ear)
- Angles: Hero (straight), 3/4, detail (macro), worn (lifestyle)

**Post-Production:**
- Color grade: Custom LUT — warm shadows, neutral highlights
- Shadows lifted slightly (not crushed)
- Highlights protected (jewelry sparkle retained)
- Dust/scratch removal: Only manufacturing flaws
- Skin texture: Preserved on hands/ears
- Metal: True color (no warming gold, no cooling silver)

**Deliverables per SKU:**
| Shot | Ratio | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Hero | 4:5 | Product page main, ads |
| Hero alt | 1:1 | Social, grid |
| Worn full | 4:5 | Product page, email |
| Worn detail | 1:1 | Product page gallery |
| Macro detail | 1:1 | Product page, zoom |
| Scale | 4:5 | Size guide |
| Packaging | 4:5 | Confirmation, unboxing |
| 360° frames | 1:1 | 360 viewer (72 frames) |

### FAMILY B: EDITORIAL — "The Story"

**Purpose:** Emotional connection, brand world-building

**Lighting:**
- Natural light only (window, open shade, golden hour)
- No artificial modification (no reflectors, no diffusion)
- Embrace: Shadows, blown highlights, color shifts
- Time of day as creative variable

**Camera:**
- 35mm full frame (24-45MP)
- Lenses: 35mm, 50mm, 85mm (prime only)
- Aperture: f/1.4-f/2.8 (shallow depth)
- Handheld. Movement encouraged.
- Film simulation: Portra 400 / Cinestill 800T aesthetic

**Subjects:**
- Real women (not models) — friends, artists, customers
- Ages: 25-55, diverse
- Styling: Their own clothes + ZARAVI
- Direction: "Forget the camera. Do what you'd do."

**Environments:**
- Personal spaces (homes, studios, gardens)
- Transitional spaces (hallways, doorways, windows)
- Nature at edges (courtyards, balconies, parks)
- Never studios, never seamless

**Composition:**
- Rule of thirds broken intentionally
- Obstacles in foreground (door frames, foliage, fabric)
- Motion blur welcome (1/30s, 1/15s)
- Jewelry discovered, not displayed
- Sequences: 3-5 images telling micro-story

**Color Grade:**
- Warm, filmic, slightly desaturated
- Highlights: Warm ivory tint
- Shadows: Deep olive tint
- Skin: Natural, warm, textured
- Grain: Added (fine, 15% opacity)

**Series Concepts:**
1. **"Morning Ritual"** — Jewelry as first adornment
2. **"The Studio Visit"** — Artists/creators at work
3. **"Travel Companions"** — Pieces that journey
4. **"Heirloom"** — Mother/daughter, grandmother/granddaughter
5. **"Transformation"** — Before/after life moments

### FAMILY C: CAMPAIGN — "The World"

**Purpose:** Brand mythology, seasonal narrative, collectible imagery

**Concept:** Each campaign = a short film in stills. One narrative arc across 8-12 images.

**Lighting:**
- Cinematic (HMI, LED, practical)
- Motivated sources (lamps, windows, fire, moonlight)
- Color temperature mixed intentionally (2700K + 5600K)
- Deep shadows, pools of light

**Camera:**
- Medium format (cropped to cinematic ratios)
- Anamorphic adapters (1.33x) for select shots
- Ratios: 2.39:1 (cinema), 4:5 (portrait), 1:1 (square)

**Production Design:**
- Custom sets / location build-outs
- Props: Antique, botanical, geological, textual
- Color palette: Strictly brand colors + neutrals
- Butterfly references: Structural, never literal

**Casting:**
- Characters, not models
- Distinctive faces, lived-in beauty
- Movement background (dancers, performers)
- International, multilingual

**Narrative Structure (per campaign):**

| Image | Role | Visual Key |
|-------|------|------------|
| 1 | Opening | Darkness, single light, silhouette |
| 2 | Inciting | Hands discovering object |
| 3 | Journey | Movement through space |
| 4 | Threshold | Doorway, window, mirror |
| 5 | Revelation | Full light, jewelry visible |
| 6 | Intimacy | Close, personal, detail |
| 7 | Connection | Two figures, shared moment |
| 8 | Transformation | Changed state, emerged |
| 9 | Aftermath | Quiet, wearing, living |
| 10 | Closure | Open door, light spilling out |

**Campaign Themes (Annual):**
- **Spring:** "Emergence" — First light, dew, unfurling
- **Summer:** "Full Flight" — Bright, bold, expansive
- **Autumn:** "Metamorphosis" — Color change, preparation
- **Winter:** "Chrysalis" — Darkness, gestation, promise

## ART DIRECTION — VISUAL UNIVERSE

### Core Visual Vocabulary

**Botanical — But Not Decorative**
- Pressed specimens (herbarium aesthetic)
- Root systems, not just flowers
- Dried, skeletal forms
- Scientific illustration style (vintage)

**Entomological — But Not Literal**
- Wing architecture (veins, scales, microstructure)
- Specimen boxes (museum display)
- Metamorphosis stages (egg, larva, pupa, imago)
- Classification charts (Linnaean)

**Material — As Character**
- Silk: Flow, reflection, translucency
- Metal: Patina, oxidation, warmth
- Stone: Strata, fracture, time
- Paper: Fiber, watermark, fold
- Skin: Pore, vein, temperature

**Light — As Substance**
- Beams in dust (Tyndall effect)
- Refraction through crystal
- Reflection on water
- Shadow as shape
- Golden hour as default mood

**Color Relationships (From Brand Palette):**

```
Dark Espresso ────────────── Warm Ivory
      │                           │
      │      Burnt Sienna         │
      └──────────┬────────────────┘
                 │
           Sage (bridge)
```

**Compositional Grammar:**

1. **Central Symmetry** — For product, campaign heroes
2. **Asymmetric Balance** — For editorial, lifestyle
3. **Vertical Division** — Two-column editorial spreads
4. **Radial Organization** — Around central object (butterfly logic)
5. **Sequential Rhythm** — Image sequences with visual cadence

## SPECIFIC APPLICATIONS

### Product Photography Standards

**Naming Convention:**
```
ZRV_[SKU]_[FAMILY]_[ANGLE]_[VARIANT]_[v#]
Example: ZRV_R-001_A_HERO_MAIN_v01
```

**File Delivery:**
- RAW (capture) + TIFF (retouched) + WebP (web)
- Color space: Adobe RGB (master) / sRGB (web)
- Naming: Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces

**Retouching Guidelines:**
| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| Metal scratches (manufacturing) | Remove |
| Metal patina (natural) | Preserve |
| Gemstone inclusions | Preserve (character) |
| Dust on product | Remove |
| Skin texture | Preserve |
| Stray hairs | Preserve |
| Background imperfections | Remove (product) / Preserve (editorial) |
| Color accuracy | Critical (metal, stone) |
| Sharpening | Output-specific (capture → web) |

### Campaign Production Specifications

**Pre-Production (8 weeks):**
- Week 1-2: Concept, moodboard, treatment
- Week 3: Casting, location, crew
- Week 4: Set build, prop sourcing
- Week 5: Wardrobe, jewelry selection
- Week 6: Lighting tests, camera tests
- Week 7: Rehearsal, shot list finalization
- Week 8: Shoot (2-3 days)

**Crew (Core):**
- Photographer/DP (same person for consistency)
- Director (narrative continuity)
- Gaffer (lighting as character)
- Production Designer (world-building)
- Stylist (wardrobe + jewelry integration)
- Hair/Makeup (natural, textural)
- DIT (color management on set)
- Producer (logistics)

**Post-Production (4 weeks):**
- Week 1: Selects, client review
- Week 2: Retouching, color grade
- Week 3: Layout, typography integration
- Week 4: Final delivery, asset library

**Asset Library Structure:**
```
/ZARAVI_Assets/
  /Campaign_[Season]_[Year]/
    /Selects/          (final 8-12)
    /Alternates/       (20-30 additional)
    /RAW/              (all captures)

    /Layouts/          (InDesign, web-ready)
  /Product_[SKU]/
    /Family_A/         (commercial)
    /Family_B/         (editorial lifestyle)
  /Evergreen/
    /Packaging/
    /Materials/
    /Details/
```

## VIDEO / MOTION CONTENT

### Film Language

**Format:** Short films (60-180s), vertical + horizontal
**Aesthetic:** Cinema verité meets fashion film
**Sound:** Original score + diegetic (no voiceover)
**Text:** Minimal, typographic, brand font

**Series:**

1. **"The Making Of"** (Quarterly)
   - 3-5 min documentary
   - One piece, one artisan
   - Process as meditation
   - End: Piece in hand, light catch

2. **"Unboxing Ritual"** (Per collection)
   - 60s real-time
   - ASMR-adjacent (paper, ribbon, box)
   - No talking
   - End: Worn, in context

3. **"Styling Sessions"** (Monthly)
   - 90s, one person, 3 ways
   - Their clothes, their life
   - Jewelry as constant
   - End: Mirror check, smile

4. **"Collector Conversations"** (Bi-annual)
   - 5-10 min interview
   - Why this piece? Why now?
   - Home, studio, archive
   - End: Favorite piece, close detail

**Technical Specs:**
- Camera: RED / ARRI / Sony Venice
- Lenses: Anamorphic (2x) + spherical
- Audio: Lav + boom + ambient
- Color: Log → Custom LUT (brand grade)
- Delivery: ProRes 4444 (master), H.265 (web), Vertical crop (social)

## VISUAL UNIVERSE EXTENSIONS

### Print / Editorial

**Lookbook (Seasonal):**
- 64-96 pages
- Perfect bound, uncoated stock
- Size: 240 × 300 mm (tall, book-like)
- No prices. Only SKUs in index.
- Essays, poetry, process images
- Collector's object.

**Journal (Digital + Limited Print):**
- Quarterly
- Long-form: Essays, interviews, photo essays
- Designed as magazine, not blog
- Print run: 500 numbered copies

**Packaging Inserts:**
- Product card: 90 × 55 mm, warm ivory, gold foil logo
- Care card: Symbol system (see Iconography)
- Certificate: 148 × 210 mm (A5), cotton paper, foil, emboss
- Thank you: Handwritten-style font, variable data

### Retail / Physical Space

**Display Language:**
- Velvet forms (not trays) — organic shapes
- Brass stands (antenna pulls as display)
- Stone bases (raw, unpolished)
- Lighting: Adjustable spots, 2700K, high CRI
- Mirrors: Antiqued, not perfect

**Window Display:**
- Single narrative per season
- One hero piece, large scale
- Butterfly reference: Structural (symmetry, emergence)
- Changes: Quarterly (with campaign)

**Scent Profile (Boutique):**
- Top: Bergamot, green tea
- Heart: Iris, violet, paper
- Base: Sandalwood, amber, beeswax
- Diffusion: Cold-air, subtle
- Never: Floral, sweet, aggressive

## QUALITY CONTROL — VISUAL

### Pre-Publication Checklist

| Check | Standard | Tool |
|-------|----------|------|
| Color accuracy (product) | ΔE < 2 vs. physical | Spectrophotometer |
| Skin tone natural | No magenta/green shift | Visual + vectorscope |
| Shadow detail | Visible to zone 2 | Histogram |
| Highlight detail | No clipping (jewelry) | Highlight warning |
| Sharpness (hero) | Pixel-perfect at 100% | 100% view |
| Composition alignment | Symmetry ±0.5% | Guides |
| Typography hierarchy | Scale ratios exact | Design spec |
| Brand color usage | Only approved values | Swatch check |
| Butterfly system | Correct level applied | System ref |
| File naming | Convention followed | Script |

### Approval Workflow

1. **Photographer** → Selects (3x final count)
2. **Art Director** → Curates to final count
3. **Creative Director** → Approves narrative/emotion
4. **Brand Director** → Approves strategy fit
5. **Production** → Technical QC
6. **Asset Manager** → Library ingest, metadata
