# 01. BRAND ESSENCE

## Brand Name
**ZARAVI**

Derived from Persian: "Zar" (gold) + "Avi" (of the air/wings). A jewelry house where precious metal takes flight.

## Brand Essence
**Transformation made tangible.**

## Brand Promise
Jewelry that marks the moments when you became who you are.

## Brand Philosophy
The butterfly is not decoration — it is doctrine. Every element of ZARAVI reflects metamorphosis: the patient emergence, the quiet reveal, the moment when something ordinary becomes extraordinary.

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Refined** | Nothing excessive. Every element earns its place. |
| **Sensual** | Tactile materials, organic forms, warmth against skin. |
| **Intelligent** | Thoughtful design that rewards close attention. |
| **Editorial** | Magazine-quality photography and typography. |
| **Artisanal** | Handcrafted feeling, small-batch sensibility. |
| **Quietly luxurious** | Expensive without announcing itself. |
| **Slightly mysterious** | Hidden details, gradual reveals. |
| **Emotionally memorable** | Pieces that become part of personal stories. |

## Brand Values

1. **Transformation over tradition** — We honor change, not convention.
2. **Materiality over decoration** — Luxury lives in materials, not ornament.
3. **Patience over speed** — Slow reveals, lasting quality.
4. **Nature over artifice** — Organic inspiration, never forced.
5. **Intimacy over scale** — Personal moments, not mass production.

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Women 28-45. Design-conscious. Values craftsmanship and story over conspicuous branding. Seeks jewelry with meaning — marking milestones, celebrating self, honoring relationships. Educated, culturally aware, environmentally mindful.

**Secondary:** Gift purchasers seeking meaningful, not generic, presents.

## Competitive Positioning

Upper contemporary to accessible luxury. Positioned alongside:
- Independent jewelry maisons (Melanie Georgacopoulos, Ana Khouri)
- Design-forward brands (Charlotte Chesnais, WWAKE)
- Contemporary heritage (Foundrae, Rey City)

# 02. BRAND POSITIONING

## Luxury Positioning

ZARAVI occupies the space where art jewelry meets wearable design. Not competing with traditional luxury houses (Cartier, Van Cleef) or fashion jewelry (Mejuri, Gorjana).

## Emotional Territory

- **Primary emotion:** Contemplative wonder
- **Secondary emotion:** Quiet confidence
- **Aspirational emotion:** Self-recognition

The ZARAVI customer doesn't buy to impress others. She buys because the piece reflects something true about herself.

## Differentiators

1. **Butterfly philosophy** — Not decorative motif, but core brand belief system
2. **Organic luxury** — Nature-inspired without being literal or bohemian
3. **Material restraint** — Fewer colors, more texture; fewer elements, more meaning
4. **Editorial quality** — Magazine-level photography and typography at every touchpoint
5. **Hidden details** — Brand rewards attention; butterfly embedded, not pasted

## Brand Story

ZARAVI began with a question: What if a jewelry brand was built not around status, but around transformation?

The butterfly is the answer. Not the pretty insect on a spring day, but the profound biological truth it represents: that the most beautiful things emerge from the most unlikely cocoons. That patience creates beauty. That change is not loss but becoming.

Every ZARAVI piece carries this philosophy. The butterfly appears not as a literal icon, but as a structural principle — in the asymmetric balance of a pendant, the way light moves across a faceted stone, the hidden signature that only the wearer knows exists.

# 07. BUTTERFLY VISUAL LANGUAGE

## System Philosophy

The butterfly is not a decoration. It is the structural DNA of ZARAVI.

The system must be recognizable even when the butterfly itself is not explicitly shown.

## Six-Level System

### Level 1: Primary Butterfly Emblem
- Abstract, geometric emblem
- Solid filled or outline (0.08x stroke)
- Used on: Seals, certificates, premium packaging
- Minimum: 8mm (print), 24px (digital)

### Level 2: Secondary Butterfly Graphics
Fragment family derived from Level 1:
- **2A:** Single wing (right-facing) — Movement, emergence
- **2B:** Wing pair (open) — Unfolding, revelation
- **2C:** Lower wing fragment — Pattern generation
- **2D:** Symmetry axis — Dividers, section breaks
- **2E:** Wing vertex — Arrow indicators, premium markers

### Level 3: Pattern System
- **3A:** Wing tessellation — Packaging paper, backgrounds
- **3B:** Facet grid — Cards, borders, texture
- **3C:** Wing vein micro — Foil details, texture
- **3D:** Emergence sequence — Animation frames

### Level 4: Micro Details
Hidden brand language:
- Blind emboss signature (inside box lid)
- Wax seal impression (gift closure)
- Jewelry engraving micro (1.5-3mm)
- Ribbon woven detail (selvedge)
- Paper watermark (stationery)
- Digital easter eggs

### Level 5: Packaging Applications
Structural integration:
- Box hinge as wing (120° opening)
- Drawer pull as antenna (brass rod)
- Ribbon closure as proboscis
- Interior geometry (facet grid)
- Gift sleeve geometry

### Level 6: Digital Motion Behavior
Motion derived from butterfly biomechanics:
- **Unfolding:** Content reveals center outward
- **Symmetry:** UI expands bilaterally
- **Vibration:** Subtle hover tremble
- **Glide:** Smooth, decelerating transitions
- **Pause:** Static states feel settled

# 11. PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

## Three Visual Families

### Family A: Product — "The Specimen"
- **Purpose:** Commercial clarity with editorial soul
- **Lighting:** Single large source, 45° above, 30° off-axis
- **Background:** Dark espresso velvet or warm ivory matte
- **Composition:** Centered symmetry, generous negative space

### Family B: Editorial — "The Story"
- **Purpose:** Emotional connection, brand world-building
- **Lighting:** Natural light only, no artificial modification
- **Subjects:** Real women (not models), diverse, 25-55
- **Environments:** Personal spaces, transitional spaces, nature at edges

### Family C: Campaign — "The World"
- **Purpose:** Brand mythology, seasonal narrative
- **Lighting:** Cinematic (HMI, LED, practical), motivated sources
- **Production:** Custom sets, narrative structure, 8-12 image series

## Photography Principles

- Light emerges from shadow (never flat illumination)
- Imperfection is intentional (evidence of life)

# 14. MOTION SYSTEM

## Motion Philosophy

Movement as Metamorphosis. Every animation tells the same story: Something emerges. Something unfolds. Something becomes.

## Logo Animation (Master)

**Duration:** 800ms

**Sequence:**
1. Symmetry axis draws downward (100ms)
2. Wings begin emerging (staggered, 200ms)
3. Wings reach apex (400ms)
4. Lower curves complete (550ms)
5. Facet notches click into place (700ms)
6. Complete — gold glow pulse (800ms)

## Page Transitions

- **Exit:** Content folds along center (300ms)
- **Pause:** 100ms (chrysalis moment)
- **Enter:** Content unfolds from center (300ms)
- **Total:** 700ms

## Image Reveals

Wing-mask reveal: SVG mask (Level 1 butterfly) expands from center (600ms, organic ease)

## Micro-Interactions

| Element | Duration | Behavior |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Button hover | 150ms | Color shift only |
| Link hover | 300ms | Underline draws center-out |
| Card hover | 300ms | Scale 1.015, wing-mask alt image |
| Form focus | 100ms | Border → burnt sienna |

## Loading States

| Loader | Use | Spec |
|--------|-----|------|
| Emergence | Page loads | 12-frame butterfly build, 720ms loop |
| Wing Flutter | Checkout | Open wings ±3° rotation, 1.2s |
| Facet Pulse | Button submit | Single diamond opacity pulse, 800ms |

# 18. DO'S AND DON'TS

## DO

- Use token references for consistency
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Embed butterfly as principle, not decoration
- Honor materials — texture and weight matter
- Create moments of reveal and discovery
- Use full typography hierarchy before adding decoration
- Show jewelry on skin for scale and warmth
- Test at all scales (jewelry engraving to store signage)
- Question: "Would this feel credible in Paris?"

## DON'T

- Mix rounded and sharp corners in same composition
- Use glossy finishes or high-shine foils
- Place butterfly randomly as decoration
- Use black as background (use dark espresso or deep olive)
- Create busy layouts that compete with product
- Use stock photography or generic luxury imagery
- Shout with typography, color, or animation
- Create unnecessary logo variations
- Add features, abstractions, or defensive code beyond requirement

# 20. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Core Principles

1. **LESS BUT BETTER.**
2. **Luxury through restraint.**
3. **Distinctive over trendy.**
4. **Materiality over decoration.**
5. **Emotion over cliché.**
6. **System over isolated pieces.**
