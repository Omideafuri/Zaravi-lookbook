# ZARAVI PACKAGING SYSTEM
*Complete Physical Ecosystem — Territory B: Dark Romantic / Editorial / Couture*

## PACKAGING PHILOSOPHY

Every ZARAVI package is a **ritual object**. The unboxing is not a means to an end — it is the first wearing of the jewelry.

**Principles:**
- Materials over decoration
- Construction as communication
- Discovery over display
- Durability over disposability
- The package outlives the purchase

## BOX SYSTEM — PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. JEWELRY BOX — SMALL (Rings, Earrings, Small Pendants)

**Dimensions (External):**
- 70 × 70 × 40 mm (W × D × H)
- Lid height: 18 mm
- Base height: 22 mm

**Dimensions (Internal):**
- 64 × 64 × 34 mm
- Usable depth: 28 mm (with tray)

**Construction:**
- 2.5mm greyboard, wrapped
- Hinged lid (integrated hinge, not separate)
- Hinge: Continuous wrap paper spine (no visible hardware)
- Opening angle: 120° (wing-inspired)
- Magnetic catch: Hidden, 3N force, at lid center front

**Exterior:**
- Full wrap: Dark espresso cotton paper
- Logo: Gold foil, centered on lid, 18mm width
- Butterfly emblem: Blind emboss, lower right quadrant, 12mm
- Edges: Clean wrapped, no visible board

**Interior:**
- Full microsuede lining (warm ivory)
- Lid interior: Blind emboss Level 1 butterfly (15mm), centered
- Removable tray: Microsuede over 2mm board
- Tray cutout: Custom per product type (see tray specs)
- Finger notch: Single, centered front

**Production Notes:**
- Wrap paper grain: Vertical (parallel to hinge)
- Glue: PVA, acid-free
- Foil stamping: 110°C, 3s dwell, medium pressure
- Embossing: 0.3mm depth, male/female die
- Magnet: Neodymium N42, 8×3mm, countersunk

## PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

### Quality Control Checkpoints

| Stage | Check | Tolerance |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Board cutting | Dimensions | ±0.5mm |
| Wrap application | Alignment, bubbles | Zero visible bubbles |
| Foil stamping | Registration | ±0.2mm |
| Embossing | Depth consistency | ±0.05mm |
| Magnet insertion | Polarity, flushness | Flush ±0.1mm |
| Ribbon threading | Symmetry, knot quality | Centered ±1mm |
| Wax seal | Impression clarity | Complete, centered |
| Assembly | Box squareness | 90° ±0.5° |
| Final | Logo alignment | Centered ±0.5mm |

### Sustainability Requirements

- All paper: FSC certified, minimum 30% post-consumer recycled
- Board: 100% recycled greyboard
- Foil: Recyclable (cold foil preferred where possible)
- Ribbon: Recycled polyester satin
- Brass: Recycled content, conflict-free
- Plastics: Zero (no poly bags, no foam, no blister packs)
- Inks: Vegetable-based, low VOC
