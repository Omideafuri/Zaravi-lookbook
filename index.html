The origin

A single butterfly wing observed under a macro lens—not as decorative ornament, but as pure architecture. A lattice of non-uniform veins distributing flight tension across an impossibly thin membrane. This structural logic is translated into solid 18k gold: the fragile mathematics of flight made permanent in precious metal.

The method

Every ZARAVI piece begins with radical subtraction. We remove ornament until only the essential tension remains—the singular curve that cannot be further simplified without relinquishing its structural soul.

The wing
is architecture.

When the wing is cropped beyond recognition, it ceases to be an insect's appendage. It becomes an engineering diagram drafted by millions of years of aerodynamic evolution—a tension grid distributing load across space.

Primary Design Anchor Reference 04

The textile/fabric wing: here the wing is expressed as material — stitched threads, woven twill, crinkled mulberry ground. This tactile embroidery inspired our hairline vein deboss grid rather than photographic printing.

Physical Metallurgy Reference 02

The physical Apex Cuff and Radial Ring in brushed 18k solid gold resting on genuine sage-green porphyry stone. Note the planar facets and deep "ZARAVI 18K" hallmark.

The Point of Departure Reference 03 (v1)

The v1 packaging used photographic wing prints as literal surface wallpaper and a symmetrical butterfly logo. In v2, we elevated this into calm monochrome surfaces, blind debossing, and the single asymmetric wing mark.

Engineering Blueprints Reference 01

Technical dielines for the friction-fit magnet box, unbleached linen pouch, and shopping tote. Inner lid quote: "YOU ARE A RARE KIND OF BEAUTIFUL".
