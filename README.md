## Overview

**ZARAVI** is an emerging international haute joaillerie maison founded on the principles of biological metamorphosis, architectural geometry, and Parisian couture restraint.

This repository houses the complete **ZARAVI Brand World**, interactive **Digital Lookbook**, and production-ready **Design Specifications System**.

---

## 🏛 Experience Architecture

1. **Brand World Explorer (`brand-world/index.html`)**
   - 12 comprehensive chapters tracing the entire brand identity from core philosophy to packaging mechanics and retail architecture.
   - Interactive Token Tuner for live chromatic calibration.
   - Global search modal (`Cmd/Ctrl + K`) spanning 23 brand touchpoints.
   - Procedural SVG fractal paper grain and fluid typography.

2. **Digital Lookbook (`zaravi-lookbook.html` & `index.html`)**
   - High-editorial immersive digital lookbook presenting Series I (*Tension & Metamorphosis*).
   - High-contrast chiaroscuro photography with full-bleed spreads, specimen vitrines, and editorial typography.

3. **Packaging Engineering & CAD Blueprints**
   - Vector dielines for the 72×72×54mm Ring Box and 178×178×44mm Sliding Necklace Drawer with calibrated 0.5mm running tolerances.
   - Full 11-stage unboxing ceremony stepper with 120° wing-stop hinge geometry.
   - Supplier-ready packaging handoff briefs and material specifications.

4. **Brand Identity System Specifications (`zaravi-brand/`)**
   - `BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`: Comprehensive master brand guidelines and quality control doctrine.
   - `DESIGN.md`: Complete design tokens, color space mappings (HEX, CMYK, PMS), typography scales, and motion curves.
   - `butterfly-system.md`: 6-level geometric hierarchy, 100×100 grid construction, and Level 2 fragment archetypes.
   - `logo-system.md`: Typographic architecture, 33% low crossbar 'A', razor bracketed serifs, clear space rules, and fiber-laser engraving tolerances.
   - `packaging-system.md`: 7-box rigid board hierarchy, greyboard calipers, unboxing force tolerances, and insert engineering.
   - `photography-art-direction.md`: Families A (Specimen), B (Story), and C (World) camera profiles and lighting protocols.
   - `stationery-retail-social-motion.md`: A5 Certificate of Authenticity, 5-zone retail flagship blueprint, and 3-2-1 social media rhythm.

## 💎 Chromatic Hierarchy (60 / 30 / 10 Ratio)

- **Mineral Sage (`#8DA28C` · PMS 5635 C · 60% Volume)**  
  Uncoated cotton paper wraps, exterior packaging, retail walls, and quiet environmental tone.
- **Warm Ivory (`#EEE9C1` · PMS 7499 C · 30% Volume)**  
  Velvet microsuede presentation pads, authentic certificates, and editorial text backdrops.
- **Burnt Sienna (`#A4530C` · PMS 1545 C · 10% Volume)**  
  Double-face satin ribbon accents, drawer pull tabs, and single-edition seal wax.
- **Dark Espresso (`#1A120B` · PMS 412 C · Foundational Ground)**  
  Monolithic ground, rigid board cores, and deep chiaroscuro voids.
