01 / Reference audit

Soft matter.
Exact structure.

The source material already owns a compelling tension: muted nature against burnt warmth, fibrous tactility against measured geometry. Its weakness is familiarity. The answer is not more ornament, but a proprietary logic.

The reference reads as premium artisanal, roughly €250 to €900. ZARAVI must shift the signal toward collector-level contemporary fine jewelry without losing touch.

03 / Strategy

Objects for the
moment after change.

ZARAVI is positioned between sculptural fine jewelry and the collectible design object, with a cultural point of view that never becomes costume.

Essence

Precious transformation

Jewelry that marks an inner shift, not merely an occasion.

Promise

A private symbol, made physical

Every piece carries a subtle duality: softness and edge, symmetry and interruption, inheritance and change.

Audience

The culturally fluent collector

Design-aware, 28 to 48, globally mobile, buys fewer and better, values authorship over status display.

Position

Contemporary fine jewelry

Above accessible demi-fine, below heritage high jewelry. Launch target: €420 to €3,800, with numbered objects above.

Values

Authorship, evidence, restraint

Visible intelligence, production truth, tactile care, long relevance and cultural specificity.

Differentiator

The archive as living system

Collections are indexed as transformations. Packaging preserves, digital reveals, and each object carries an archival notation.

Voice

Precise, intimate, unhurried

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Sensory verbs. No praise adjectives where an observable detail will do.

Tagline

Wear what changed you.

Alternates: Objects of Becoming. / Kept in Motion. / Evidence, in Gold.

Manifesto
Not every transformation asks to be seen. Some live close to the skin. A line turns. A surface remembers pressure. Two halves never return in quite the same way. ZARAVI makes evidence of becoming.

05 / Typography

Editorial display.
Archival detail.

Bodoni Moda supplies optical elegance without faux heritage. Manrope handles product truth. Vazirmatn carries Persian with matching calm and clean numerical rhythm.

Form
holds
memory.
Display / Bodoni Moda

Sentence case, optical size on.

Headlines: 48 to 152 px, line-height 0.82 to 1.0, tracking -0.055 to -0.02 em. Never set long copy in display.

Information / Manrope

Body: 16 px / 24 px. Labels: 11 px uppercase / 0.12 em. Product titles remain sentence case. Specifications use tabular numerals.

10 / Photography

Three lenses.
One atmosphere.

Natural grain, controlled warmth and real surfaces bind every image family. Skin remains skin. Metal retains micro-scratches. Shadows have direction.

Product

85 to 100 mm macro, f/8 to f/11, chalk or porous olive ground, cross-polarized where necessary, accurate metal.

Editorial

50 to 85 mm, shallow focus, cropped gesture, north-window light, silk and skin, one imperfect interruption.

Campaign

35 to 50 mm, sculptural environment, human traces, wing geometry in shadow or architecture, never literal costume.

13 / Competitive stance

Respect the codes.
Break the sameness.

Category conventions worth keeping: immaculate product truth, calm navigation, exact provenance and generous space. Conventions to reject: default monochrome, empty “timeless” copy, ornamental monograms and interchangeable campaign faces.

Completedworks owns sculptural wit. Alighieri owns literary talisman. Charlotte Chesnais owns fluid geometry. ZARAVI should own transformation as an archival, material system.
01

DoIndex collections, number objects, reveal process and let packaging carry narrative.

02

Do notCompete through louder luxury cues, vague empowerment or copied editorial austerity.

03

Long-term testIf the butterfly vanished, the folds, veins, language, palette and archive logic would still identify ZARAVI.
